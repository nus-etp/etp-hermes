# Tacniq — LIVING BRIEF
_Last updated: 2026-10-04 20:11 UTC_
![Infographic](infographic.png)

## Thesis
Tacniq is a Singapore-based deep-tech startup building AI-powered tactile sensors and software that let machines interpret physical contact, with its Backy workplace-safety wearable deployed at DHL achieving over 65% reduction in posture-related risk. In September 2026 it raised US$1.5M from In Group Holdings within a US$3M pre-seed round to fund engineering hires, model development and commercial deployments, and is co-developing deployable tactile-AI hardware with Synaptics as it scales beyond its BLOCK71 Singapore base.

## Recent signals
- **2026-09-23** — Singapore's TacniQ raised US$1.5M from In Group Holdings within a US$3M pre-seed round to build tactile-sensor AI, and is co-developing commercially deployable tactile-AI hardware with Synaptics — [apac.entrepreneur.com](https://apac.entrepreneur.com/business-news/singapores-tacniq-raises-1-5-mn-to-build-ai-that-learns-from-touch)
  - Summary: TacnIQ.ai said it secured US$1.5M from Singapore investment firm In Group Holdings as part of a US$3M pre-seed round, with the money earmarked for engineering hires, model development and more commercial deployments; the announcement disclosed no valuation, named no other investors and did not clarify whether the larger round had closed. The startup builds touch sensors and AI software in Singapore and California, collecting data from workplace wearables and robotic sensors to train reusable models. It says its Backy workplace-safety wearable uses pressure and tilt sensors with vibration alerts, its TAC-02 robotic finger development kit carries 64 touch-sensing elements, and it has collected more than 5,000 hours of tactile interactions with paying customers across logistics, construction, e-commerce, hospitality and healthcare. It is also working with US-listed semiconductor firm Synaptics on turning tactile-AI research into commercially deployable hardware.
  - People: Aashish Mehta (CEO & co-founder, TacnIQ.ai), Liu Song (CEO, In Group Holdings), Mahesh Srinivasan (VP & GM Touch & Display, Synaptics)
  - Counterparties: In Group Holdings (lead investor), Synaptics (technology partner)
  - Numbers: US$1.5M from In Group Holdings (part of a US$3M pre-seed); no valuation disclosed; 5,000+ hours of tactile data; 64 touch-sensing elements (TAC-02 kit)
