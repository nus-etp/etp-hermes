# MADCash — LIVING BRIEF
_Last updated: 2026-09-16 17:33 UTC_
![Infographic](infographic.png)

## Thesis
MADCash is a Malaysia-based Shariah-compliant fintech (HQ Kuala Lumpur) providing micro-funding, financial literacy and digital tools to women micro-entrepreneurs across Malaysia, Singapore and Tajikistan, with backing from partners including Bank Islam, Maybank and Yayasan Hasanah. In July 2026 founder Nuraizah Shamsul Baharin disclosed the company's own operating revenue for the first time — RM1.2M, up 139% year on year — alongside a shift from pure zero-interest lending to a cost-plus Murabahah model so the platform earns revenue from lending instead of depending on CSR and charity grants, targeting break-even this year and profitability by 2027. It has disbursed more than RM4M to over 1,500 women entrepreneurs and is raising RM10M of Murabahah funds to reach 2,000 more.
