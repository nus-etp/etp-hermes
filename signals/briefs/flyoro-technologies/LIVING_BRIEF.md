_Last updated: 2026-09-02 17:10 UTC_

## Thesis
Flyoro Technologies is a Singapore-based startup developing AlphaLite, a sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) blending system. In June 2026 it signed a memorandum of understanding with Menzies Aviation to explore deploying AlphaLite at European airports served by Menzies, leveraging Menzies' fuel farm management and into-aircraft refueling operations — a potential European distribution channel for its SAF technology.

  - Summary: The MOU pairs Aether Fuels' Aether Aurora technology — which converts industrial waste gas and biomethane into CORSIA-certified SAF, with Project Beacon designed for up to 50 barrels per day (~2,000 tonnes/year), >70% lifecycle emissions cuts versus conventional jet fuel, construction expected in 2026 and commercial operations targeted for 2028 — with FlyORO's modular AlphaLite blending platform. FlyORO's role is downstream enablement: assessing how flexible, on-demand blending could complement Project Beacon and future Aether projects. The release also documents FlyORO's commercial track record: AlphaLite, described as the world's first modular SAF blending system, was deployed in 2025 with Wagner Sustainable Fuels and Boeing at Toowoomba Wellcamp Airport in Queensland, Australia — the first SAF blending terminal co-located at an airport globally — advancing it to Technology Readiness Level 9, and FlyORO had blended more than 500,000 litres of SAF across deployments as of May 2026. The MOU is non-binding and non-exclusive; funding and team size remain undisclosed.
  - People: Jonathan Yeo (Chief Executive Officer, FlyORO), Conor Madigan (Founder & Chief Executive Officer, Aether Fuels)
  - Counterparties: Aether Fuels (partner), Wagner Sustainable Fuels (prior deployment partner), Boeing (prior deployment partner)
  - Numbers: up to 50 bpd / ~2,000 t/yr at Project Beacon; >70% lifecycle emissions cut; 500,000+ litres SAF blended; TRL 9 after Wellcamp
