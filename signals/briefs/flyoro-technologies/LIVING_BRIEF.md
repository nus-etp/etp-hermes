# Flyoro Technologies — LIVING BRIEF
_Last updated: 2026-09-05 15:57 UTC_

## Thesis
Flyoro Technologies is a Singapore-based startup developing AlphaLite, a sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) blending system. In June 2026 it signed a memorandum of understanding with Menzies Aviation to explore deploying AlphaLite at European airports served by Menzies, leveraging Menzies' fuel farm management and into-aircraft refueling operations — a potential European distribution channel for its SAF technology.

The release also documents FlyORO's commercial track record: AlphaLite, described as the world's first modular SAF blending system, was deployed in 2025 with Wagner Sustainable Fuels and Boeing at Toowoomba Wellcamp Airport in Queensland, Australia — the first SAF blending terminal co-located at an airport globally — advancing it to Technology Readiness Level 9, and FlyORO had blended more than 500,000 litres of SAF across deployments as of May 2026. The MOU is non-binding and non-exclusive; funding and team size remain undisclosed.

Through this MOU, we want to explore how smarter downstream infrastructure can complement next-generation SAF production and help make supply more flexible, transparent and practical for the local market." — Jonathan Yeo, Chief Executive Officer, FlyORO
