# Marymount Labs — LIVING BRIEF
_Last updated: 2026-09-20 16:46 UTC_
![Infographic](infographic.png)

## Thesis
Marymount Labs is a Singapore digital-health startup building 'Broadcast', an AI-powered WhatsApp engagement platform that automates patient outreach across smoking-cessation, Hospital@Home triage, and preventive-care campaigns. NUS Medicine's Digital Health Accelerator (DATA) states the team drew pre-seed funding through the programme and has moved beyond patient activation to autonomous AI care workers, deployed across GP practices and Active Ageing Centres with pilots at Singapore public healthcare institutions.

_Last material event: 2026-08-13 — NUS Medicine's DATA accelerator discloses pre-seed funding for Marymount Labs_
