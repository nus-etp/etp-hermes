## Thesis
Marymount Labs is a Singapore digital-health startup building 'Broadcast', an AI-powered WhatsApp engagement platform that automates patient outreach across smoking-cessation, Hospital@Home triage, and preventive-care campaigns. The Health Ministry named its WhatsApp Companion as a showcase project at the SUSS Geronpreneurship Innovation Festival, highlighting deployment with the Tzu-Chi Foundation at Active Ageing Centres.
