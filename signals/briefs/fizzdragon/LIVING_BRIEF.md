# FizzDragon — LIVING BRIEF
_Last updated: 2026-09-11 17:06 UTC_
![Infographic](infographic.png)

## Thesis
FizzDragon is a Singapore-headquartered AIGC company building an integrated platform that generates scripts, characters, and cinematic scenes from a single workflow — and producing its own feature films on that stack. Its AI-generated sci-fi feature “Trace Project” premiered in Singapore on National Day 2026 before release on the FizzDragon App, with distribution confirmed in Malaysia and the US, following an earlier 130-creator feature, “Pirate Queen: Zheng Yi Sao”.
