_Last updated: 2026-08-04 15:23 UTC_

## Thesis
Singapore-based quantum computing software company building Triple Alpha, a hardware-agnostic integrated development environment, and operating its own quantum hardware testbed, Ember-1. Founded 2018 by Joe Fitzsimons, the company is now publicly listed as Horizon Quantum Holdings (Nasdaq: HQ) and reported Q2 2026 results with $113.3M in cash, moving its Beryllium programming language into early access as it pushes toward broad quantum advantage.
