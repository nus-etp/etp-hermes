# ConcreteAI — LIVING BRIEF
_Last updated: 2026-11-01 21:16 UTC_
![Infographic](infographic.png)

## Thesis
ConcreteAI is an NUS GRIP-incubated Singapore deep-tech startup whose IoT sensors and analytics track in-situ concrete strength and temperature in real time, replacing century-old cube testing on construction sites. Tech in Asia's October profile puts it at 60+ projects across Singapore, Malaysia, Australia and Bahrain, with LTA, Changi Airport Group and CDL among its clients — a commercial footprint well beyond what its 2022 seed round funded — and says the company plans its next raise for 2027.

_Last material event: 2026-08-04 — Selected as an APAC Pitch Day finalist in the Construction Startup Competition 2026_
